Open to close is the market's opinion changing, not the model's. Fair already is the open — the feed carries Pinnacle's opening price and nothing re-quotes it, so every run of a day stores the same fair for a side and there is no intraday move to show. close is the closing line and Line move is close − fair in points, positive when the market came towards this side after it opened and the open was the better price. That is closing line movement, and it is deliberately not called CLV: closing line value is the close minus what was actually paid, a different and flattering number, because §2 rests a cent under fair and that discount alone is worth about a point. CLV in that sense lives on the Kalshi page, split from the discount. Both are blank until the match is graded and the close arrives, usually a day later, and blank for a void: a price a market was suspended at is not its last word. Line move is the honest scoreboard while the settled sample is still too small to read — it says whether you were on the right side of a moving line, on every bet rather than on the handful that have finished. ROI is model ÷ price − 1, the expected return per unit at that price. quote is Pinnacle's raw vigged price, fair is the devigged open, and −1c/−2c are one and two points inside fair — the prices you would actually type into Novig. Placing an order moves the row onto the Book, which is where the venue, stake and fill get recorded.

Kalshi take is the same ROI if you cross the spread at fair on Kalshi instead of resting on Novig: model ÷ (fair + fee) − 1, with the taker fee 0.07 × p × (1−p) added to what you pay. It is the answer to "is it still worth it if I just take it now". Hover a cell for the fee in cents. The fee is a curve, not a haircut — it peaks at 1.75c at a coin-flip price and falls to about 1.1c at 0.20 or 0.80, so it eats a far larger share of a thin edge in the middle of the board than at the edges. Against the 2.5-point floor that is roughly 70% of the edge gone at 0.50 and 45% at 0.20. The maker fee is a quarter of it, 0.0175 × p × (1−p), which is why the frozen rule posts rather than takes.
